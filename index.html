Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to move my furniture?

I just ask that you move smaller items and anything fragile before I start. I work alone, but I'm happy to move appliances and some furniture like a dining table. If you have a lot to move, or really heavy items, just let me know so we can plan ahead.

How long does it take?

Small projects, like a single room, usually take a day or two. Larger jobs, like the main floor of your house, may take up to a week.

What areas do you serve?

Kent County, including Grand Rapids, Wyoming, Kentwood, Walker, Grandville, East Grand Rapids, Cascade, Ada, Forest Hills, Lowell, Alto, Caledonia, Byron Center, Cutlerville, Gaines Township, Dutton, Rockford, Belmont, Comstock Park, Plainfield Township, Northview, Grand Rapids Township, Eastown, East Beltline, Standale, Cannonsburg, Bostwick Lake, Whitneyville, Alaska, and nearby areas.

Do you install carpet or hardwood?

No. I specialize in LVP, and I'd rather do one thing really well than a lot of things okay.
